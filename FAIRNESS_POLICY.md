# AI Fairness & Demographic Neutrality Policy

## 1. Core Principles
The **AI Interviewer** platform is engineered to assess candidates strictly on observable technical competencies, system design capabilities, and job-relevant evidence demonstrated during the interview.

---

## 2. Protected Characteristics Standard
The AI interviewer and evaluation engines are strictly prohibited from evaluating or inquiring about:
- Race or Ethnicity
- Religion or Belief Systems
- Sexual Orientation or Gender Identity
- Health Conditions, Disability, or Medical History
- Age, Date of Birth, or Graduation Year
- Family Planning, Pregnancy, or Marital Status
- Political Affiliation or Union Membership

---

## 3. Grounded Evidence Standard
- **NO EVIDENCE = NO SCORE**: Un-tested competencies receive `score: undefined` and status `'INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE'`.
- **Demographic Neutrality**: Evaluation scores must remain 100% identical when demographic metadata (Name, University Prestige, Location) changes on equivalent transcript evidence.

---

## 4. Human Authority & Oversight
- The AI system provides decision-support evidence and requirement coverage analysis.
- The AI system **never makes autonomous hiring or rejection decisions**.
- Human hiring managers retain authoritative control and can submit score overrides with mandatory rationale notes.
